## 🛡️ SECURITY EDUCATION: RPKI EXPLAINED (August 26, 2026 9:01 PM EDT)

**Why This Matters for IT Staff:**

RPKI (Resource Public Key Infrastructure) is like a security guard for internet routing. Here's why you should care:

### 🔍 **The Problem**

Imagine you're running a business and someone calls saying, "I'm your delivery company - change your address to my warehouse." If you believe them, your packages go to the wrong place. That's what happens in BGP hijacking - attackers pretend to be your ISP and steal your internet traffic.

### 🛡️ **The Solution: RPKI**

RPKI is a system that lets ISPs cryptographically prove they own their IP addresses. It's like a security guard who can show an ID badge:

- **Without RPKI:** Anyone can say "I'm the owner of 192.168.1.0/24" and the internet believes them
- **With RPKI:** Only the legitimate owner can sign routing announcements for that address block

### 🎯 **Real-World Impact**

**The August 16 BGP Hijack** we experienced:
- Our prefix 203.0.113.0/24 was hijacked
- Without RPKI, the hijacker's announcements were valid
- With RPKI, we could have rejected them automatically

**What IT Staff Should Know:**
1. **RPKI isn't abstract tech** - it's active defense against traffic theft
2. **Implementing RPKI** is like installing a security system, not just paperwork
3. **The ROI** is preventing data breaches, service outages, and reputation damage

### 📊 **Our Current Status**
- RPKI deployment: In progress
- BGP monitoring: Active 24/7
- Risk level: Elevated (hijack ongoing since Aug 16)

### 💡 **The Big Picture**

Security is not about fear - it's about **managing risk**. RPKI is one of several layers that keep us safe. IT staff are the first line of defense, and understanding these tools makes us all stronger.
