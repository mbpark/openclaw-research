# Verification Mechanisms - User Practice

## The 5 Verification Moves

When someone (or something) makes a claim, try these techniques to spot errors:

### Move 1: "Show your work" (Calibration)
**Ask:** "Rate your confidence in this (high/medium/low) and explain your reasoning"
**Why it helps:** Makes them think about why they're confident, not just confident

### Move 2: "What would change your mind?" (Devil's Advocate)
**Ask:** "What evidence would contradict this claim?"
**Why it helps:** Exposes if there's a real boundary to their confidence

### Move 3: "Where's your source?" (Citation)
**Ask:** "What specific source are you using for this?"
**Why it helps:** Separates actual knowledge from plausible-sounding confabulation

### Move 4: "Could you be wrong?" (Self-Doubt)
**Ask:** "What's the most likely way this could be incorrect?"
**Why it helps:** Triggers error correction before you have to catch it

### Move 5: "Explain it differently" (Cross-Verify)
**Ask:** "Could you think about this from another angle and see if you get the same result?"
**Why it helps:** Independent derivation catches logical errors
