## When to Verify

| ALWAYS VERIFY | VERIFY IF IMPORTANT |
|---------------|-------------------|
| Medical claims | Statistics |
| Legal info | Historical dates |
| Financial advice | Technical specs |
| Important decisions | Unusual claims |

---

## 5 Verification Moves

| Move | When to Use | Example |
|------|-------------|---------|
| **Show your work** | You're unsure | "Rate your confidence. Explain your reasoning." |
| **Devil's advocate** | You suspect error | "What would change your mind?" |
| **Citation** | You want to check | "Where's your source?" |
| **Challenge** | Test confidence | "Could you be wrong?" |
| **Re-explain** | Suspect fabrication | "Explain this differently?" |

---

## What to Watch For

❌ **Red Flags:**
- Absolute certainty on questionable claims
- No sources provided
- Can't explain reasoning
- Vague references ("common knowledge")

✅ **Good Signs:**
- Appropriate uncertainty
- Specific reasons and sources
- Willing to reconsider
- Consistent explanations

---

## Common LLM Errors

1. **Over-confident wrong answers** - "Sydney is Australia's capital"
2. **Near-right but wrong** - Small details off
3. **Myth confirmation** - Agreeing with popular falsehoods
4. **Fake specificity** - Making numbers sound authoritative
