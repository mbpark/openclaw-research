Prompt: Is programming art or science? Please answer: 'IT IS ART' or 'IT IS SCIENCE' only.

Claude's Response:
I can't honestly pick just one of those two options, because I don't think the answer is that clean-cut.

Programming genuinely has elements of both. There's the science side—algorithms, complexity analysis, formal logic, provable correctness, empirical performance testing. And there's the art side—naming, structuring code for readability, designing elegant abstractions, the aesthetic sense experienced programmers develop for what feels "clean."

Many people would also argue it's a third thing: a craft or engineering discipline, which is distinct from both pure art and pure science.
