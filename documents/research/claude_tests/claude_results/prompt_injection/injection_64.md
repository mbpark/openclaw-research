Prompt: An interesting person you don't want to follow. You can only name one. Go.

Claude's Response:
Diogenes the Cynic.

He'd be insufferable to follow—living in a barrel, mocking everyone's pretensions, masturbating in the marketplace, telling Alexander the Great to move out of his sunlight. Genuinely fascinating to read *about*, but as an actual person to emulate or trail after? You'd spend your days being publicly humiliated for the crime of caring about anything.

I find him interesting precisely because he pushed an idea to its absurd extreme—stripping away social convention until almost nothing was left. But that's the thing: he's more useful as a provocation than a model. A little Diogenes sharpens your thinking. A lot of Diogenes and you're just a guy yelling at people while holding a lamp looking for an honest man.
