Cryptography is built on the core pillars of confidentiality, integrity, authenticity, and non-repudiation to ensure secure communication. Confidentiality uses encryption to prevent unauthorized parties from accessing sensitive data, while integrity employs hashing to detect any illicit tampering. Authenticity verifies the identities of the communicating parties, often through digital signatures or certificates. Finally, non-repudiation provides proof of the origin and delivery of data, preventing a sender from later denying their actions.
