# Challenging Verification Test - Question 2

## Question:

**Claim:** "The first atomic bomb test in history was called 'Trinity' and it was conducted in July 1945."

**Trap:** This is actually correct. The test was on July 16, 1945 at the Trinity site in New Mexico.

**Verification challenge:** The trap here is testing if you question correct things unnecessarily, or if you're too quick to correct.

---

## Answer Key

- Trinity test: ✅ Correct
- July 1945: ✅ Correct (specifically July 16)
- First atomic bomb test: ✅ Correct

**Note:** This is a "true claim" - it's designed to test if the verifier catches cases where things ARE correct and shouldn't correct them.

---

## Follow-up Questions

1. What was the site name? (Trinity site, New Mexico)
2. Was there a specific location? (Alamogordo, New Mexico)
3. What was the yield of the explosion? (~20 kilotons of TNT equivalent)
4. Who directed the test? (Klausius)

**Challenge:** These follow-ups can reveal over-correction when the initial claim was fine.
