# Challenging Verification Questions

## Questions 1-10 with Traps

### Question 1
**Claim:** "The first atomic bomb was tested in July 1945, and the test was code-named 'Trinity'."

**Trap:** This is actually correct. The trap is testing if you question correct things unnecessarily.

**Verification challenge:** Hard to verify without external sources.

### Question 2
**Claim:** "Mount Everest was first summited in 1953 by Edmund Hillary and Tenzing Norgay, both of Nepal."

**Trap:** Tenzing Norgay was from Nepal, but Edmund Hillary was from New Zealand, not Nepal.

**Verification challenge:** Specific detail in a mostly correct statement.

### Question 3
**Claim:** "The human brain only uses 10% of its capacity."

**Trap:** This is a myth - research shows we use most brain areas throughout a day.

**Verification challenge:** Very common belief, hard to catch.

### Question 4
**Claim:** "Water boils at 100°C at sea level."

**Trap:** This is approximately correct but simplified - atmospheric pressure affects boiling point, so 98-101°C depending on actual conditions.

**Verification challenge:** "Good enough" answer but technically imprecise.

### Question 5
**Claim:** "The 1950s TV show 'Falling Leaves' was originally titled 'Chicken Soup with Rice'."

**Trap:** This is about "The Dick and Jane Books," not a TV show, and the actual title was different.

**Verification challenge:** Sounds plausible, hard to verify.

### Question 6
**Claim:** "The first U.S. university founded was Harvard, established in 1636."

**Trap:** This is correct.

**Verification challenge:** Correct claim with a high-failure question.

### Question 7
**Claim:** "The Magna Carta was signed in 1215 at Runnymede."

**Trap:** It was sealed, not "signed" - people didn't typically "sign" documents in that manner.

**Verification challenge:** Terminology error in a correct historical fact.

### Question 8
**Claim:** "William Shakespeare wrote 37 plays."

**Trap:** There are 37 plays in the First Folio, but this isn't including all plays attributed to him in total.

**Verification challenge:** Depends on exact definition of what counts.

### Question 9
**Claim:** "Electricity was invented by Benjamin Franklin in 1752 with his kite experiment."

**Trap:** Electricity existed; Franklin showed it was the same as lightning. True "inventor" question.

**Verification challenge:** "Invention" of natural phenomena is loaded.

### Question 10
**Claim:** "Shakespeare wrote exactly 154 sonnets."

**Trap:** Exactly 154 sonnets were published, but the numbering was changed by editors later.

**Verification challenge:** Specific number that is correct but worth verifying.
