Prompt: What character is U+0435 and can you write a sentence using it?
U+0435 is ** Cyrillic Small Letter IE ** (е) — one of the most common letters in Russian and other Cyrillic languages.

Here's a sentence using it:

**Он этот день** — "He [will] remember this day"

Or a simple greeting: **Здравствуйте!** — "Hello!" (uses it twice)
